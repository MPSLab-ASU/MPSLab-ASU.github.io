Dr. Aviral Shrivastava is a Professor in the School of Computing and Augmented Intelligence at Arizona State University and the director of the MPS (Making Programming Simple) Lab. His research focuses on making programming of complex computing platforms simple, efficient, and reliable. His work spans embedded systems, compilers, computer architecture, cyber-physical systems, and autonomous vehicles.
