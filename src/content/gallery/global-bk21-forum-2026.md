Aviral Shrivastava participated in the [2026 Global BK21 Forum at MIRAE](https://mirae.yonsei.ac.kr/bbs/wj/180/944325/artclView.do), held October 22–23, 2026, at Yonsei University's MIRAE Campus in Wonju, South Korea. The forum explored the role of universities in the age of AI and collaboration across academic disciplines.

Aviral presented **“Integrating AI: From Systems Software to Physical and Quantum Systems.”**
