## Machine Learning Acceleration

Deep learning models are becoming increasingly complex, requiring specialized hardware to achieve the necessary performance and energy efficiency. Our research focuses on:

- **Hardware-Software Co-design**: Developing novel architectures and compilation techniques that work together to optimize neural network inference and training.
- **Edge AI**: Designing efficient accelerators for resource-constrained edge devices, enabling intelligent applications without relying on cloud computation.
- **Dataflow Optimization**: Creating tools to automatically determine and execute the most efficient dataflow mapping for specific neural network models on spatial accelerators.

We aim to make deploying advanced machine learning models simple, efficient, and accessible across a vast range of computing environments.
