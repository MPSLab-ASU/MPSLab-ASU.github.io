## Intelligent Transportation Systems

The future of mobility relies heavily on the safety, reliability, and intelligence of software systems in vehicles. At MPS Lab, we are tackling the most pressing challenges in autonomous driving:

- **Perception Pipeline Optimization**: Improving the efficiency and accuracy of the software pipelines that process sensor data to understand the vehicle's surroundings.
- **Safety-Critical Architectures**: Designing computing architectures that can guarantee the strict timing and reliability requirements necessary for autonomous operation.
- **Hardware Acceleration for AVs**: Utilizing specialized hardware to process complex driving scenarios with minimal latency.

Our work contributes to the development of safer and more efficient intelligent transportation systems, bridging the gap between advanced algorithms and practical vehicular computing platforms.
