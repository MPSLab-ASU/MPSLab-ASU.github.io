## Quantum Machine Learning

Quantum computing promises exponential speedups for specific classes of problems. At MPS Lab, we are investigating how quantum algorithms can revolutionize machine learning:

- **Quantum Neural Networks**: Designing and simulating neural network architectures that operate on quantum principles.
- **Quantum Optimization**: Utilizing quantum algorithms to solve complex optimization problems that are critical to training large-scale machine learning models.
- **Hybrid Algorithms**: Developing practical approaches that combine classical computing resources with quantum processors to achieve near-term advantages.

Our research in Quantum Machine Learning aims to prepare the groundwork for future-ready AI systems capable of tackling problems that are intractable for today's supercomputers.
