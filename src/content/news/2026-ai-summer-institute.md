This week, our lab, in collaboration with Rio Salado College, hosted the AI & Semiconductor Summer Institute, 2026. Sponsored by Intel and the National Science Foundation (NSF), this immersive program focused on empowering K-12 educators to navigate the complex nuances of Artificial Intelligence and connect them directly to semiconductor industry investments. Our goal was to provide teachers with the tools and knowledge necessary to help their students discover and pursue high-growth career pathways in these cutting-edge fields.

KJZZ 91.5 FM covered the event, and an article highlighting the program was published. Read more about the event coverage here: [Link](https://lnkd.in/giTBTi3C)
