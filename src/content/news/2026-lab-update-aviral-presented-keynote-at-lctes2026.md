Dr. Aviral Shrivastava delivered a keynote on "Practical Quantum Machine Learning" at the ACM SIGPLAN Conference on Languages, Compilers, and Tools for Embedded Systems (LCTES 2026), held in conjunction with PLDI. Addressing the severe hardware noise and vanishing gradients of the Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) era, his presentation introduced Quantum-Projected Metric Learning (QPMeL). This paradigm-shifting approach bypasses current optimization bottlenecks by reframing QML entirely as a metric learning problem. By shifting training optimization to classical computers and eliminating noisy entangling layers, QPMeL requires just two gates per qubit—exponentially improving hardware scaling while achieving state-of-the-art multi-class accuracy.
