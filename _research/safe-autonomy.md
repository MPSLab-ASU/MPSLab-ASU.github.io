We create software and hardware techniques that keep robots and autonomous vehicles reliable even under tight deadlines. Our work spans safety monitors for perception, certification-ready middleware, and hardware acceleration for low-latency decision making.

Key thrusts include:

- End-to-end timing analysis and schedule synthesis for autonomy stacks
- Runtime safety monitors that guard perception and planning
- Accelerator-aware software that keeps performance predictable in the field
